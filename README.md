# Cybersecurity Risk Assessment Report
This project was completed as part of the Datacom Cybersecurity Job Simulation hosted by Forage.

## Objective
The goal of this assessment was to analyze cybersecurity incidents affecting a fictional retail organization, RetailNova, evaluate organizational risk exposure, and recommend appropriate security controls to mitigate future threats.

## Scenario
RetailNova experienced three significant cybersecurity incidents over a three-year period:

1. Credential compromise resulting from a phishing campaign targeting employees.
2. Ransomware activity that caused temporary operational disruption.
3. Third-party vendor data breach exposing customer email addresses.

A detailed risk assessment was conducted to identify root causes, assess business impact, classify risk levels, and recommend remediation strategies.

## Project Objectives
- Analyze cybersecurity incidents and their impact.
- Identify organizational security weaknesses.
- Assess overall cybersecurity risk.
- Develop mitigation and remediation recommendations.
- Produce a professional risk assessment report.

## Risk Assessment Summary
| Incident | Risk Level |
|-----------|------------|
| Credential Compromise (Phishing) | Medium |
| Ransomware Attempt | High |
| Third-Party Vendor Data Leak | Critical |

## Security Recommendations
- Implement Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA).
- Strengthen email security controls and phishing protection.
- Conduct mandatory cybersecurity awareness training.
- Improve network segmentation.
- Enhance vendor risk management processes.
- Encrypt sensitive customer data.
- Regularly test incident response and disaster recovery procedures.
